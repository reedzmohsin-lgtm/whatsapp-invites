2. Host & settings

This app is running on your own computer, so guests can't open the invite page (calendar, map and RSVP buttons) yet. Put the app online (see README), enter the address above, and the invite links will work. The Google Calendar and map links in the message work either way.

3. Guests

Picking from Contacts only works in Chrome on Android. On iPhone or a computer, export your contacts as a vCard (.vcf) or CSV file and upload it. On a Mac: Contacts → select contacts → File → Export → Export vCard.

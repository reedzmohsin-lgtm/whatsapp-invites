3. Invitation card

How card sending works: on your phone, Send opens the Share menu. Choose WhatsApp, then pick the guest. The message is also copied, so if WhatsApp drops the text, just paste it in. On a computer the card is copied: open the chat from the pop-up, press ⌘V (Ctrl+V on Windows) to paste the card, then send.

4. Extra files (optional)

Add photos, a poster, a PDF agenda or a video to send with every invite.

No files added.

    Files are sent through the Share menu, which sends them with the text in one go. Photos, videos and PDFs work best. If a file type can't be shared, it's downloaded for you to attach with 📎. Files stay in this browser only.

    5. Host & settings

    This app is running on your own computer, so guests can't open the invite page (calendar, map and RSVP buttons) yet. Use the online version, or enter its address above.

    6. Guests

    Picking from Contacts only works in Chrome on Android. On iPhone or a computer, export your contacts as a vCard (.vcf) or CSV file and upload it. On a Mac: Contacts → select contacts → File → Export → Export vCard.
